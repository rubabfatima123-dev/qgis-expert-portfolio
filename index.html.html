SEVERITY: CRITICAL Task ID: QGIS-AI-AUDIT-042
Domain: Spatial Distance & CRS Transformation

Flawed AI Output: Geographic Coordinate Euclidean Calculation

The AI Claim: The model calculated least-cost path distance directly on unprojected WGS84 coordinates (EPSG:4326, degrees) by treating longitude and latitude delta values as linear meters.

Error Identified: Ignoring longitudinal meridian convergence at higher latitudes results in severe metric distortion (up to 30%+ error in distance matrices outside the equator).
Corrected Methodology for AI Training: All raster/vector inputs must be reprojected to an appropriate local UTM or equal-area projected CRS prior to Euclidean or cost-distance processing.
SEVERITY: MODERATE Task ID: QGIS-LIT-REV-108
Domain: Spatial Autocorrelation & Citation Quality

Literature Review & Moran's I Interpretation Audit

The AI Claim: Summarized a spatial epidemiology paper by stating that a global Moran's $I = +0.52$ proves uniform local clustering across all regional administrative zones.

Error Identified: Conflating global spatial autocorrelation summary statistics with local spatial regimes (LISA / Anselin, 1995). Global metrics mask localized hot-spots and outliers.
Corrected Methodology for AI Training: Clarified that global Moran's $I$ indicates overall spatial structure, whereas Local Indicators of Spatial Association (LISA) must be cited for cluster-level evaluation.
